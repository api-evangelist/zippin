Zippin and Yellow Dog Software are teaming up to take checkout-free shopping to the next level for sports teams and venue operators. This partnership integrates Yellow Dog’s proven back-office and inventory management tools with Zippin’s industry-leading checkout-free technology. By combining these two systems, operators will benefit from a more comprehensive view of store inventory and performance, while making their checkout-free store operations more efficient.
