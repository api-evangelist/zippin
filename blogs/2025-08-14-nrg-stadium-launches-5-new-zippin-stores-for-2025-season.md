Just in time for the 2025 NFL season, NRG Stadium has once again expanded its partnership with Zippin, adding five new checkout-free stores and more than doubling its existing frictionless footprint. The venue now operates nine Zippin-powered stores in total—one of the largest deployments of autonomous retail in professional sports.
