This Thanksgiving, air travel is set to hit new heights. AAA projects that 5.84 million people will fly domestically during the holiday —a 2% increase over last year and an impressive 11% jump from pre-pandemic levels in 2019. International travel is booming too, with bookings up 23% as lower fares tempt holidaymakers abroad.
