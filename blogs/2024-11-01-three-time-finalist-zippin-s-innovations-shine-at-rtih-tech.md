At Zippin, our commitment to leading the checkout-free retail experiences has once again earned us industry-wide recognition. For the second consecutive year, we’re finalists in three categories of the prestigious Retail Technology Innovation Hub (RTIH) Tech Awards . This year’s shortlist places us at the forefront of the Customer Experience Excellence, Technology Implementation of the Year - UK, and Technology Implementation of the Year - Rest of World categories.
